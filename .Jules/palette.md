## 2026-05-24 - Accessible Toggle Switches & Async Button Indicators
**Learning:** Using `display: none` on standard checkbox/radio inputs inside custom toggles or components completely hides them from screen readers and keyboard tab navigation. Replaced with a utility "visually hidden" clip styling, the elements remain accessible in the DOM tree, focusable, and compatible with keyboard interactions. Additionally, slow SMTP/PDF actions like timesheet approvals must have clear immediate loading states (`data-loading-text` + animated SVG) to prevent duplicate clicks/submissions and indicate background work.
**Action:** Always avoid `display: none` for underlying interactive form elements. Use a standard "visually hidden" layout and style the `:focus-visible` state of custom pseudo-elements or sibling indicators (like `.toggle-slider`). Ensure all slow network operations have clear visual loaders.
