## 2025-06-01 - Accessible and Timing Adjustable Flash Alerts
**Learning:** Flash alert and toast popups must be both accessible to assistive technologies (roles like role="alert" or role="status") and adjustable/pausable for timing-related accessibility requirements.
**Action:** Always make sure critical alerts do not auto-dismiss without user interaction, and non-critical alerts can be paused on focus or hover so users have sufficient time to read and act upon them. Also add visible focus-visible indicators for close/dismiss buttons.
