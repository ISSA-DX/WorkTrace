## 2025-05-24 - Accessible Flash Alert Announcements and Keyboard Focus States
**Learning:** Dismissible notification alerts rendered dynamically in Flask Jinja templates often use icon-only close buttons (like `✕`) without an explicit accessible label or live-region role, leaving screen reader users unaware of status feedback and unable to understand the dismiss control. Adding `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` to alert banners along with `aria-label="Dismiss alert"` and `:focus-visible` outline styles ensures instant announcement and seamless keyboard navigation.
**Action:** When working on notification or banner components across the app, always verify that the container is an ARIA live region (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`), icon-only close buttons specify `aria-label`, and keyboard focus styling (`:focus-visible`) provides high contrast outlines using existing theme tokens.
