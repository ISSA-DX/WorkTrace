## 2026-05-24 - [Accessible Custom Toggle Switches]
**Learning:** Hiding checkbox elements with `display: none` completely removes them from the accessibility tree, making it impossible for screen-reader users to interact with or even find them, and prevents keyboard users from focusing on them. Visually hiding the native input with a clipped boundary (`clip: rect(0,0,0,0)`) preserves its interactive state and focus-ability in the tab index, allowing developers to style a custom switch slider via next-sibling selectors and apply `:focus-visible` styles to the custom element.
**Action:** Always visually hide underlying interactive inputs using screen-reader-safe properties rather than `display: none`, and bind `:focus-visible` states of hidden elements to highlight their custom styled wrappers.
