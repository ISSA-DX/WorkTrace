## 2025-10-25 - Visually Hidden Inputs over display:none
**Learning:** Using `display: none` on inputs (such as checkboxes or radio buttons used for styled custom toggle switches) completely removes them from the accessibility tree. This prevents screen readers from announcing them and makes it impossible for keyboard-only users to focus on them via tabbing. Modern accessible designs should always keep the inputs interactive by visually hiding them off-screen while supporting keyboard focus navigation via `:focus-visible` styles on their companion labels or styled wrappers.
**Action:** Replace `display: none` styled inputs with a robust, absolute-positioned, clipped visual hiding utility class/rule. Then, use `:focus-visible` selectors to apply focus rings on styled custom components to ensure keyboard users have visual affordances during navigation.
